Customer Context

Tier-1 Commercial Aviation Actuation Subcontractor supplying critical hydraulic subsystems for widebody aircraft flight control assemblies.

The Business & Engineering Challenge

The component required a monolithic Inconel 718 hydraulic housing requiring ±0.0003" concentricity across 14 intersecting micro-bores. The legacy supplier suffered severe tool chatter, high scrap rates exceeding 14%, and chronic delivery delays.

Vanguard's Engineering Solution

Vanguard's engineering team re-engineered the CAM strategy into a single-setup 5-axis process utilizing custom PCD tooling, high-pressure cryogenic liquid nitrogen cooling, and harmonic vibration damping fixtures designed in-house.

Documented Manufacturing Outcome

Machining cycle time dropped from 142 minutes to 88 minutes per piece. The program attained a 99.85% first-pass acceptance across all 4,200 delivered units with zero FAA conformity escapes.
